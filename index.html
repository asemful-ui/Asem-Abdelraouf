Welcome to my homepage!

I am a Simons Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at the Kyiv School of Economics, working with Masha Vlasenko. Previously, I obtained my Ph.D. in Mathematics at SISSA, jointly with ICTP, under the supervision of Fernando Rodriguez Villegas. Before that, I did a master's degree in Mathematics at the univeristé of Paris, and I did the ICTP Postgraduate Diploma in Mathematics.

My research lies in arithmetic and algebraic geometry, in particular the theory of hypergeometric motives. These are the arithmetic counterparts of the classical hypergeometric functions of Euler and Gauss. They occur in the cohomology of families of algebraic varieties, and counting points on these varieties over finite fields gives finite hypergeometric sums. I work on realising hypergeometric motives in explicit families of varieties and on computing their point counts and zeta functions.

  • Hypergeometric motives
    • Geometric realisations of hypergeometric local systems
    • Point counts on toric hypersurfaces over finite fields
  • p-adic methods
    • Frobenius lifts and supercongruences
  • Harmonic analysis over finite fields
    • Stability of optimal extension inequalities
