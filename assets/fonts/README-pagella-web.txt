Pagella Web
===========

The pagella-web-*.woff2 files are derived from TeX Gyre Pagella, version 2.501,
by the GUST e-foundry (https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre),
distributed under the GUST Font License (see GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt).

Changes: converted from OpenType (texgyrepagella-*.otf) to WOFF2 and subset to
the Latin character ranges used on this website. As requested by the license,
the derived files carry a new name. The glyph designs are unchanged.

They are used as a fallback for visitors whose systems do not have Palatino.
